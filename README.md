My mobile game created for the js13kgames competition.

You are a hunter, and you must survive endless waves of unicorns seeking to pollute the forest and destroy you. Use your shotgun to eliminate the unicorns and your green decontaminator gun to cleanse the trees.

CONTROLS

Movement: touch the left side of the screen
X: Fire shotgun or decontaminate
Z: Switch item between weapon and decontaminator
